Security and responsible disclosure

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in a Kumobits AB website or service, please tell us. We welcome reports made in good faith.

How to report

  • Email hello@kumobits.com with "Security" in the subject line.
  • Describe the issue, the affected URL or component, steps to reproduce and the impact you expect.
  • Reports in English or Swedish are welcome.

What we ask

  • Act in good faith and only test what is in scope.
  • Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, and stop as soon as you have shown the issue.
  • No denial of service, social engineering, spam or physical testing.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public disclosure.

What we commit to

  • We confirm receipt within 5 business days.
  • We keep you informed while we investigate and fix the issue.
  • We will not pursue legal action against research done in good faith under this policy.
  • With your permission we credit you when the fix is published.

Scope

In scope: kumobits.com and its subdomains operated by Kumobits AB. Out of scope: client systems, which belong to their owners, and third party services. Report issues in those directly to their owners.

Machine readable contact details: /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).
