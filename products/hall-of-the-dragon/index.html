About

Hall of the Dragon is a calm VR hall built for Meta Quest 3. You sit, a mentor waits by the table, and lessons teach you to shape the elements with spoken power words and simple hand gestures.

It is designed to be played without controllers. Hands, voice and gaze all work, and controllers stay optional. Sessions are short, the type is large and the motion is calm.
