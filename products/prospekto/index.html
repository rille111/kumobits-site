What it does

Prospekto is a Kumobits tool for teams who need named decision makers in marketing and sales, with a Nordic focus.

You can search in Swedish and English by name, title, company, or country. Import and export CSV or XLSX. Rows without a source URL are skipped, and so are duplicates.

You can watch companies, get notified of hits, and recheck whether someone is still in the role. A live source check is not switched on in the current pilot.

A private pilot is running now. It is not an open signup. Write to rickard@kumobits.com if you want to talk about access.
