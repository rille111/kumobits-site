About

Orbit Siege puts you in orbit around a planet under attack. Hold off escalating waves, and between waves pick one of three upgrades that build on each other. Every fifth wave a boss tests your build.

A second player can drop in at any time on the same device, by keyboard, gamepad or a second touch. The planet's health is shared.
