About

Kumo Körkort is a theory app for the vehicles generic driving apps barely cover: electric scooters, mopeds and electric cars.

The same e-scooter can legally be a bicycle, a class II moped or a class I moped, and your duties change with the class. The app starts with a simple classifier based on the official limits from Transportstyrelsen, with links to the official pages.

Next comes an electric car module on things like quiet vehicles and pedestrians, and regenerative braking in winter. Practice questions will be our own, never copies of official exam items.

Features

What it does

Which class is my scooter?

Bicycle up to 20 km/h and 250 W, class II up to 25 km/h and 1 kW, class I up to 45 km/h.

Official sources

Every rule links to Transportstyrelsen.

Electric car module

Quiet vehicles, regenerative braking and winter driving.

Original practice questions

Written by us. Not official Trafikverket exam items.

Swedish first

Built for Swedish rules and Swedish readers.

One codebase

Flutter for Android and iOS from the same code.
