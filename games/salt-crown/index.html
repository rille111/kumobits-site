About

The Salt Crown is low fantasy, mud and stone, politics first. A cold coastal realm of rival houses, where honour runs into pragmatism and winter is a season and a threat.

The first playable slice is one place: the keep at Saltmere at dusk. Walk the yard, climb to the parapet and speak with Captain Corv by the armory and Lady Iskra Fen on the wall. Your standing with the Tide, the Reed and the Salt starts out untried.

The prototype uses placeholder art. The target look is cinematic: wet slate at low sun, banners in a hard wind and portraits that carry as much weight as the map.
