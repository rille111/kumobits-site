About

Neon Drift is an endless racer on a neon grid under a synthwave sun. Weave through traffic, hold a drift, then release it for a burst of speed.

Every day there is a new seeded course, the same for everyone. Arcade mode keeps a fixed course. Your personal best comes back as a ghost to race against.

It runs in the browser on desktop and on phones, with no install.

Features

What it does

Drift to boost

Hold Space while steering, then release to boost.

Daily course

A new seeded track every day at 00:00 UTC, plus a fixed Arcade course.

Ghost replays

Race your personal best. Records and ghosts stay in your browser.

Near misses and pickups

Traffic, boost pickups and close calls for extra score.

Touch ready

On phones, on screen steering with automatic throttle, plus Brake and Drift buttons.

Synthesized sound

Engine sound and music are generated in code with Web Audio.
