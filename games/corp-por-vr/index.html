About

Corp Por VR is a small playable concept for one idea: in a fantasy world where spells have always had spoken words, you should be able to say them out loud.

Hold the trigger, aim at the daemon and say "Corp Por". An Energy Bolt strikes. Say "Vas Flam" for a fireball. If the daemon's fire hits you while you cast, the spell fizzles and half the mana is lost.

It is the playable demo behind our Ultima Online VR pitch, a proposal for bringing that world to Quest 3 with voice casting at its core.

Features

What it does

Voice casting

Hold the trigger, aim and speak the words. Push to talk, so table talk never casts.

On device speech on Quest

Quest Browser has no speech API, so the demo runs Vosk right on the headset with a small grammar of sound alikes.

Forgiving matching

Fuzzy matching on the letters, so "core poor" or "copper" still cast Energy Bolt.

Real spell rules

Taking a hit while casting makes the spell fizzle, true to the classic rules.

Desktop mode

Mouse and keyboard in Chrome or Edge. Hold Space and speak, or press 1 and 2 to cast without voice.

Light on hardware

Add ?lowfx to the address to turn shadows off on weaker devices.
