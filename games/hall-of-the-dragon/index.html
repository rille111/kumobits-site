About

Hall of the Dragon is a calm VR hall built for Meta Quest 3. You sit, a mentor waits by the table, and lessons teach you to shape the elements with spoken power words and simple hand gestures.

It is designed to be played without controllers. Hands, voice and gaze all work, and controllers stay optional. Sessions are short, the type is large and the motion is calm.

The first lessons are the Art of Air: aim at a ribbon and make the cloth lean.

Features

What it does

No controllers needed

Power words, hand gestures and gaze. Controllers are optional, never required.

Seated by design

Built for a chair: calm motion, short sessions and a Rest button that is always there.

Normal and Advanced

Normal mode keeps gestures slow and gentle. Advanced mode opens the fuller weaves and movement.

Light and dark themes

Switchable themes, readable in the headset and on a flat screen.

A guide in the hall

A spoken, skippable first run guide inside the game.

Pluggable recognizers

Hands and speech are plugins, so a better recognizer can drop in without rebuilding the hall.
