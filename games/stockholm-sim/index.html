About

Stockholm Sim is a calm open city, seen as blocks. Streets, water and roofs come from open map data and are turned into voxels. Characters are pixelized and fictional.

You start on foot in Gamla stan. Walk, look around, or take the car parked nearby. Day turns to night, and you can pause whenever you want.

The first slice is one district, not all of Sweden. Combat is not part of it.

Features

What it does

A real city, as blocks

Gamla stan rebuilt from OpenStreetMap footprints, one metre cells, courtyards kept.

Walk or drive

First and third person on foot, plus an arcade car with headlights.

Day and night

A day and night cycle and a pause that never punishes you.

Normal and Advanced

A calm Normal mode for the first run, Advanced later for fuller play.

Readable

Large text, light and dark themes, and a short skippable guide.

Controls

Keyboard, mouse and gamepad. VR and phone controls are planned.
