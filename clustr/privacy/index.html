Clustr privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Clustr ("the extension") is a Chrome extension by Kumobits that sorts your browser tabs into named, colored tab groups.

Summary

  • Kumobits does not receive your data. Clustr has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.
  • With no AI provider set up (the default), nothing leaves your browser.
  • If you add your own AI provider, Clustr sends only tab titles and hostnames to that provider, and only when tabs are sorted.

What the extension reads

To group tabs, Clustr reads the title and URL of the tabs in the window you are sorting. Pinned tabs and non-web pages (such as chrome:// pages) are skipped. Clustr does not read page content, cookies, form data, passwords or your browsing history.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and is never synced to your Google account:

  • your settings;
  • your API key, if you add one;
  • the result of the last sort, shown in the popup;
  • a small cache of which group each hostname was put in, so fewer AI calls are needed. Entries expire after 7 days by default. You can change that or clear the cache in Options.

Removing the extension deletes all of this data.

What is sent, and to whom

Only if you pick an AI provider in Options and save your key or URL, Clustr sends the following each time tabs are sorted:

  • a short tab number, the tab title (cut to 120 characters) and the hostname (for example booking.com) for each tab being sorted;
  • the full URL, without login details or the # part, only if you turn on "Send full URLs" (off by default);
  • the names of your existing groups, so they can be reused.

This goes only to the provider endpoint you chose:

  • Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com),
  • DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com),
  • xAI (api.x.ai), or
  • the OpenAI-compatible base URL you typed in, such as OpenRouter, Groq, or a local server on your own computer.

Your API key is sent only to that same provider, to authenticate the request. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what you send it. Kumobits is not a party to that exchange and never sees it.

Clustr asks Chrome for access to a provider's website only when you turn that provider on, and you can withdraw access at any time at chrome://extensions.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your data.
  • We never send data to Kumobits or to anyone except the provider you chose.
  • We never use your data for advertising, profiling, credit checks or anything other than grouping your tabs.
  • We never load remote code.

Children

Clustr is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children.
