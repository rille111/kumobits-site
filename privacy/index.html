Privacy policy

This policy explains how Kumobits AB processes personal data in connection with this website and our work. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers, third party scripts or third party fonts.

Controller

Kumobits AB, org. no. 559158-2258, Stockholm, Sweden. Contact: hello@kumobits.com.

What data we process

  • Correspondence. When you email us we process your name, email address, any other contact details you include and the content of your message.
  • Client work. Contact details of people at clients and prospective clients, needed to plan and deliver an assignment.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider processes technical request data, such as IP address, time, requested page and browser information, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Answering and handling correspondence: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Preparing and performing client assignments: performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before entering into one (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Bookkeeping and invoicing: legal obligation under Swedish accounting law (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Operating and securing the website: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

Hosting provider

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. acts as our hosting provider and processor and processes request logs on our behalf. Request logs are kept according to GitHub's standard retention. GitHub may process data outside the EU and EEA under the safeguards set out in its data processing terms.

Retention

  • Correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle the matter and any follow up, and is then deleted.
  • Client related data is kept for the duration of the assignment and as long as needed afterwards for the agreement.
  • Accounting records are kept for seven years, as required by the Swedish Bookkeeping Act (Bokföringslagen).
  • Server request logs follow GitHub's standard retention.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to data portability. To use these rights, email hello@kumobits.com.

Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), imy.se.
